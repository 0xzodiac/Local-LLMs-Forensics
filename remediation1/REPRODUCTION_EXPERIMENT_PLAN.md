## Falsifiable outcomes

- If the full mitigation does not reduce residue against the positive-control-calibrated scanner, the complete-sanitization claim is falsified.

- If KV cells are zeroed while heap strings remain recoverable, report mechanism-specific KV protection rather than prompt confidentiality.
- If timing AUC cannot be regenerated byte-for-byte from retained rows and script, leave the timing claim unresolved.
