# Replication documentation

## Replication layers

### Primary research execution

The original LLAnalyzer experiments produced the retained local reports, JSON
records, vectors, telemetry directories, figures, and manuscript summaries.
Their provenance is uneven: selected rows are machine-generated and
recomputable, while RQ1 headline campaign records and timing vectors are not
fully retained.

### Collaborative cross-machine rerun

The author confirms that Dr. Mohammed Ikram completed a complete LLAnalyzer
rerun on a separate research server and reported matching results across the
complete framework. This is important research history and should be reported;
it is not correct to say the framework was never replicated.

The local workspace does not contain the Ikram server manifest, binary/model
hashes, raw output, or analysis bundle. Therefore the classification is
**AUTHOR-REPORTED MATCH**, not **VERIFIED MATCH**. No third-party independent
reproduction is claimed.

### Independent archival recomputation

The historical-evidence audit recomputed selected local results: memory
aggregate arithmetic, direct slot transfer, continuation counts, Wilson
intervals, and KV benchmark means. This verifies calculations from retained
records; it does not recreate a model-backed experiment or inspect the Ikram
server.

### Secondary reviews

P. K. Sharma's article recomputed percentages and Wilson arithmetic, checked
documentation and source context, and discussed policy/scope issues. The Korean
technical discussion summarized the same preprint. Neither is an execution
manifest or independent experimental reproduction.

## What to write

Use “author-reported full cross-machine collaborative rerun” or “reported
collaborative replication.” Use “independently recomputed from retained local
records” only for the selected arithmetic. Reserve “artifact-verified
cross-machine replication” for a later evidence state in which source, binary,
model, run, raw result, and analysis links have been inspected.

## What not to write

- Do not assign exact Ikram numbers from the general match statement.
- Do not say the rerun was bit-for-bit identical.
- Do not call Sharma a third-party reproducer.
- Do not treat missing local Ikram files as evidence that no rerun occurred.
- Do not remove RQ1--RQ4 because server-side artifacts are unavailable.
