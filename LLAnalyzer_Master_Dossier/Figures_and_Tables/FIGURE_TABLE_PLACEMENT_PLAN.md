   supplement if it crowds the causal/provenance explanation.
8. **Table `tab:vram`:** RQ2.4 or supplement, depending on final page pressure.

   a historical report and not locally recomputed.

## Supplement

Place hardware, software, evaluated-model, experiment-scale, detailed residue,
and statistical tables in the supplement. Add a replication-status table with
three evidence layers: original records, independent local recomputation, and
author-reported Ikram rerun.

## Reuse policy

Reuse existing source files and generated artifacts. Do not redraw a figure to
make an unsupported result appear more precise. If a plot cannot be linked to a
raw vector or a clearly labeled historical report, keep it as a historical
illustration or omit it after author review.
